**Our Vision:** ArcusX exists to solve the human crisis of unemployment and financial exclusion by creating accessible economic opportunities for people worldwide, regardless of their location, banking status or financial situation. We believe everyone deserves the chance to earn income, support their families and improve their quality of life through meaningful work. **Our Objectives:** Build a decentralized freelancing platform on Stellar that connects clients with workers through secure escrows, enabling instant payments, fair dispute resolution and transparent ratings. Create a system where workers can earn income and manage debt without intermediaries or barriers, empowering individuals to take control of their economic future. Launch on Stellar Mainnet to bring this solution to people globally, demonstrating how blockchain technology can create genuine human impact by connecting people and enabling economic opportunities that transform lives, not just generate transactions.
