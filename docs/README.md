## What is ArcusX?

**ArcusX** is a Web3 platform that connects users with paid microtasks, helping people earn income remotely through transparent, decentralized, and low-barrier systems. Designed with the needs of underserved communities in Latin America in mind, we are building digital bridges to real economic opportunities.

ArcusX is a decentralized freelancing platform built on the Stellar blockchain that connects clients with workers through secure escrow smart contracts. We offer low fees (0.5%), instant payments (3-5 seconds), and global access to freelance opportunities.
