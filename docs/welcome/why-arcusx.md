# Why ArcusX?

ArcusX is a decentralized freelancing platform built on the Stellar blockchain that revolutionizes how clients and freelancers collaborate through intelligent escrow smart contracts.

## Key Advantages

### 💰 Ultra-Low Fees

ArcusX charges only 0.5% commission per transaction, significantly lower than traditional platforms that charge between 10% and 20%. This cost reduction benefits both clients and workers.

### ⚡ Instant Payments

Stellar transactions confirm in 3 to 5 seconds, eliminating the long waiting periods associated with traditional bank transfers. Payments are processed immediately once the client approves the completed work.

### 🔒 Secure and Decentralized Escrow

The escrow system uses Trustless Work smart contracts on Stellar, ensuring funds remain secure until both parties agree. The platform cannot freeze, hold, or interfere with funds deposited in escrow.

### 🌍 Global Access

There are no banking restrictions or geographical limitations. Anyone with internet access and a Stellar wallet can participate on the platform, regardless of location.

### 🔍 Complete Transparency

All transactions are verifiable on the Stellar blockchain. Every payment, deposit, and fund release is permanently recorded on a public and immutable ledger.

### ⚖️ Dispute Resolution

The system includes an integrated dispute resolution mechanism with administrative oversight. In case of conflicts, an administrator reviews the situation and can resolve the dispute by fairly distributing funds.

## Cutting-Edge Technology

ArcusX leverages Stellar's infrastructure, a blockchain proven in production with over 8 years of continuous operation. Stellar processes millions of transactions daily and has demonstrated its reliability and scalability.

Trustless Work smart contracts provide an additional layer of security, ensuring transactions execute exactly as programmed without possibility of manipulation.
