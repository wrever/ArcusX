# Compliance

ArcusX is committed to operating in compliance with applicable laws and regulations. This document outlines our compliance approach and user responsibilities. ✅

## Regulatory Compliance

### Financial Regulations

As a blockchain-based platform facilitating payments:

- We comply with applicable financial regulations
- We implement Know Your Customer (KYC) procedures where required
- We monitor for suspicious activities
- We report as required by law
- We cooperate with regulatory authorities

### Data Protection

We comply with data protection regulations:

- **GDPR**: European General Data Protection Regulation compliance
- **CCPA**: California Consumer Privacy Act compliance
- **Other Regional Laws**: Compliance with applicable regional privacy laws
- Data minimization and purpose limitation
- User rights and data access

### Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

We implement AML measures:

- Transaction monitoring
- Suspicious activity reporting
- User verification procedures
- Risk assessment and management
- Compliance with AML regulations

### Counter-Terrorist Financing (CTF)

We comply with CTF requirements:

- Screening procedures
- Transaction monitoring
- Reporting obligations
- Compliance with CTF regulations
