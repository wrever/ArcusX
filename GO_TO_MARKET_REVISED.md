# Go-to-Market (revisado con México)

**Pregunta:** How users discover, try, and buy.

---

## Respuesta sugerida (copy-paste) — GTM real

**Discover:** Direct channels and community—Stellar ecosystem (events, Discord, X), content and word of mouth in LATAM. We start in Chile to validate, then focus on Mexico and Argentina (markets with adoption in progress; we have contacts from Stellar travel to support visibility there). No paid acquisition or partners yet.

**Try:** Product-led—free signup, connect wallet (Freighter or other Stellar wallet), browse or create tasks on testnet. No paywall to try; first task and first payment demonstrate the value (fast settlement, low fees) with minimal friction.

**Buy / Adopt:** Users “buy” by completing a first job or paying a first freelancer; Stellar is the payment rail (escrow, payouts in seconds). Value proposition is clear in LATAM: banking barriers, remittances, and fast, low-cost payouts. We grow through usage and referrals before adding paid channels or partnerships.
