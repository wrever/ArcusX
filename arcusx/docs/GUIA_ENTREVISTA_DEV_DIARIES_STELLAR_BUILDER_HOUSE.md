## 2) Explain what you are building?

**Short answer (EN):**

**Extended answer (EN):**

## 4) Why microtasks specifically instead of traditional freelancing jobs?

**Short answer (EN):**  
Because microtasks solve immediate bottlenecks. Companies don’t need a full hire for every small technical need.

**Extended answer (EN):**  
Traditional freelancing often starts with long vetting and broad scopes. Microtasks are narrower, faster to evaluate, and easier to complete with clear acceptance criteria. For companies, that means lower friction and faster output. For builders, that means quicker cycles, clearer expectations, and consistent income opportunities.

## 6) Can you discuss the tech stack and what it’s built on?

**Short answer (EN):**

**Extended answer (EN):**

We use escrow mechanics via Trustless Work to structure task-based settlement. The architecture is evolving toward stronger reliability, identity layers, and enterprise-grade workflow controls.

### 6a) How does your escrow system work?

**Short answer (EN):**  
Funds are committed to escrow and only released when agreed task milestones or completion conditions are met.

**Extended answer (EN):**  
The core principle is conditional release. The client funds escrow, task progress is tracked through workflow states, and release happens after approval. This reduces trust risk for both sides: companies know payment is tied to delivery, and workers know funds are reserved for completion.
